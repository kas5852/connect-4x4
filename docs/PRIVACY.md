# Connect 4x4 privacy policy

Updated October 5, 2026.

Connect 4x4 stores offline game progress and settings on your device so you can resume a match. Ending an offline match clears its saved progress. Removing the app removes its local app data.

Online play uses Apple's Game Center. The app uses the players' game-scoped Game Center identifiers to establish their roles and displays the opponent's Game Center name. During a match, the phones exchange moves, board state, turn deadlines, and clock-calibration messages through Game Center. These messages support the live game. The app does not save online matches to its offline storage or operate a developer-owned server that retains them.

Apple operates Game Center authentication, matchmaking, and networking under [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). You can use Solo or Local play without choosing Online.

The app includes no advertising, third-party analytics SDK, developer-managed user account, or tracking system. It does not request access to your contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or location. It does not sell user data.

The source code and support are available in the [Connect 4x4 GitHub repository](https://github.com/kas5852/connect-4x4). You can contact the maintainer through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/kas5852/connect-4x4/issues); anything you post there is public and is handled by GitHub.
